Smart Parking Management System

ParkaApp

ParkaApp is a SaaS platform for smart parking management that digitalizes and centralizes parkings, spaces, clients, vehicles, occupations and payments.

ParkaApp is a SaaS platform for smart parking management, developed with ASP.NET Core 10 and Entity Framework Core. It allows managers to centralize all their parking operations: parkings, zones, spaces, clients, vehicles, occupations and tariffs.

The solution includes a dashboard to follow availability, occupancy rates and revenue. It aims to simplify daily operations, improve financial follow-up and modernize the management of parking infrastructures.

The goal is to offer parking owners and managers in Madagascar a modern solution, with centralized management, activity follow-up and better control of revenue.

Problem-Solving Overview

From identifying issues to delivering solutions with impact.

Problem

  • Parking operations managed manually
  • Spaces, clients and vehicles tracked separately
  • No clear view of occupancy and revenue

Causes

  • No centralized system for parkings and spaces
  • Parking fees calculated by hand
  • Occupations and payments not tracked together

Consequences

  • Management errors and revenue losses
  • Time wasted on daily operations
  • Decisions taken without reliable statistics

Solution

  • Centralized platform for parkings, zones and spaces
  • Entry / exit tracking with automatic fee calculation
  • Hourly, daily and monthly tariffs
  • Dashboard with occupancy and revenue

How ParkaApp Works

From parking setup to revenue tracking in nine simple steps.

01

Configure the parking

The manager sets up the parking and its zones.

02

Register the spaces

Parking spaces are created and organized inside each zone.

03

Register clients & vehicles

Clients are recorded and their vehicles are linked to them.

04

Record the entry

When a vehicle arrives, its entry is registered.

05

Assign a space

A parking space is attributed to the vehicle.

06

Track the duration

The system follows the parking time automatically.

07

Calculate the amount

At exit, the amount to pay is computed from the tariff.

08

Track the payment

The payment is recorded and followed in the system.

09

Update the figures

Statistics and revenue are refreshed in the dashboard.
